Evidence 01

Survival Meters

Bottom-left HUD cluster. Four circular meters arranged diagonally against the underwater environment. No panel behind them—fully transparent background.
Original crop — 358×152px from sn07_1131.jpg
Observed:
  • Large O₂ circle (~2.4× diameter of small circles) with bright green/aqua border (#4ade80), white "O₂" and "225" inside.
  • Three smaller circles arranged diagonally to the left: red-bordered circle (#e85d4a) contains a white ring with dark center (eye/donut shape); yellow-bordered circle (#d4a017) contains a white arc segment on dark fill (gauge); blue-bordered circle (#4ecdc4) contains a light blue teardrop.
  • All have dark translucent fills. No rectangular panel behind the cluster.
  • Text is white, sans-serif, centered.

Evidence 02

Vehicle Status Panel

Bottom-right HUD when piloting a vehicle. Two separate translucent panels with organic bullet-shaped left ends. Strong cyan glow bloom on borders.
Original crop — 410×152px from sn07_1131.jpg
Observed:
  • Two separate blue translucent panels with lighter cyan borders. Both have smooth bullet-shaped left ends (rounded teardrop/point) and rounded right ends.
  • Top panel is larger, containing wrench icon + white "63" and yellow lightning bolt + yellow "89".
  • Bottom panel is smaller, containing thermometer icon + yellow "14°C". Indented offset from left edge of top panel.
  • Gap of ~3px between panels. No hard corners.
  • Strong outer glow bloom (engine post-processing, not CSS-box-shadow). Translucent glass-like interior with specular highlight on top edge.

Depth & Compass

Top-center HUD strip. Curved compass arc with depth numbers above and cardinal directions below. Two-toned arc (cyan left, yellow right).
